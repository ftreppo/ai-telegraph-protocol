# Code of Conduct

This project expects respectful, constructive collaboration.

Contributors should:

- discuss ideas and implementations rather than attacking people;
- assume good faith while remaining precise about technical risk;
- welcome disagreement supported by examples, tests or reasoning;
- avoid harassment, discrimination and personal abuse;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation when necessary to keep collaboration productive and safe.
